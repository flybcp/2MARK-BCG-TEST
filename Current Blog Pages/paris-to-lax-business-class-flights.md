## Connecting Flights are Cheaper

A direct / non stop flight is the ideal situation for most business travelers. There are a few benefits when choosing a flight with a stopover. If you are planning a vacation in the United States, it may be worthwhile making a stop in New York for a few days, before continuing onto California.

The first item to check is for the total flight duration. Depending on the airline and the connecting airport, a stopover can be as short a 90 minutes. When the savings is thousands of dollars, many times a connection is worth considering. The usually connection point for CDG to LAX flights is a New York airport like Newark or JFK. Occasionally, there are flights the have a stopover in Atlanta, when flying with Delta.

## Business Class Flight Types

There are 3 types of business class flights, and surprisingly, the fares are pretty much the same. So it is important before booking your flight, to get some in-depth knowledge of the types of Business Classes.

### Cheap European Business Class

Many Europeans already know, the business class on flights like those between France and Italy, is just an economy seat, with the middle seat blocked. That is not worth much, since you can easily pay for your seat and choose a window / aisle based on your personal preference.

### American Business Class Flights

When flying in the continental United States, you flight can be as long as 6 hours, or as short as 30 minutes. For example from EWR to LAX the flight time is 6 hours. Major Airlines have recently upgraded their planes, along with special names for Business Class. Each airline has their own unique names for the various class of service. JetBlue makes it simple with their Mint Class. Delta on the hand offers: Delta One, Delta Premium Select, First Class, Delta Comfort Plus. Which one of those is actually the business class you are looking for?

### International Business Class Flights

When traveling transatlantic or transpacific, many people assume they will be getting a lie flat seat in business class. This is not always the case. Many airlines are still flying planes with business class seats that are not fully flat. An airline like Etihad offers a world class Business Class, with unique seating configurations for couples and single travelers. Their cabins offer seats in both directions, so that you are not tripping over your neighbors feet. It is very important to consult with your travel coordinator, to be sure you are getting the business class experience you are expect.

Currently, there are three airlines offering you a direct nonstop flight from Paris to LAX. Air France, Delta and Ait Tahiti Nui. Since Delta & Air France are code share partners, many of their flights are actually the same. When booking with points, Air France is on the SkyTeam Airline Alliance, so points from airlines like Delta and KLM may be useable.
