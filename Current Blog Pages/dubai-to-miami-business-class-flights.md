# Dubai to Miami Business Class Flights

**URL:** https://bestaltitude.com/dubai-to-miami-direct-flights/

Emirates Airlines started flying from Dubai to Miami, direct non stop flights on July 22nd 2021. The flights to Miami operate four times a week on the luxurious Emirates Boeing 777-300ER aircraft. The sweetest part of this new service are the 42 lie flat seats offered in the Business Class section! As you know, we all love flying business class, and now we have plenty of seats to choose from.

The new route is called Emirates flight number EK213, which departs from Dubai International Airport (DXB) at 3:10 AM and arrives in Miami International (MIA) at 11:00 AM. When flying from Florida to the U.A.E., the flight number is EK214. Flights depart from Miami at 9:10 PM, and arrives in Dubai the next day at 7:35 PM.

## Flying Dubai to Miami Business Class

While there are usually other options for direct nonstop flights between New York and the Dubai in the United Arab Emirates, currently Emirates Airlines is the only one showing up. Emirates flies the popular Boeing 777 airplane on their new DXB to MIA route. If you haven't seen Emirates Business Class, take a few minutes and see the virtual tour.

Business Class seats on Emirates are completely lay flat. Their website shows that they are configured as 2 – 3 – 2, which means the middle seat in the middle of the plane, has a person on both sides. For some travelers this is a problem, however, when traveling as a party of three, this can be alot of fun.
