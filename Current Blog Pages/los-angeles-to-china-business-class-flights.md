# Los Angeles to China Business Class Flights

**URL:** https://bestaltitude.com/los-angeles-to-china-business-class/

A business class flight, non stop from Los Angeles to China (Shanghai), takes just over 12 hours. Flights with 1, 2 or 3 stops will add many hours of travel time to the trip. Ideally, a direct / non stop flight is the ideal situation for most people. However, there can be benefits of taking a flight with a stopover.

When planning your trip to China, you need to know that there are over 30 major airports in China. Most people know of Beijing and Shanghai, China is a huge country, and your conference / expo may be hundreds of miles away.

## Business Class from Los Angeles to China

Typically, we will find many options for nonstop Business Class flights to China. Currently, the best option for a direct flight is with Air China or China Southern via Vancouver. Air China flies the popular Boeing 777, and they call the business class seats "Capital Pavilion". That sounds alot better than their First Class which is called the Forbidden Pavilion. The Business Class seats are a nice size of 75 inches (6.2 feet) long and 21 inches wide. They are practically lie flat at a technical 170 degrees. If you remember from Geometry class in 10th grade, 180 degrees is a straight line. Air China is a Star Alliance member airline.

## Types of Business Class

There are different types of business class sections of airplanes, and many times the price is the same for lower quality. It is smart before buying your ticket, to gain some depth knowledge of the various types of Business Class.

### Domestic American Business Class

When flying in the continental United States, you flight can be as long as 6 hours, or as short as 30 minutes. Major Airlines have recently upgraded their planes, along with special names for Business Class. Each airline has their own unique names for the various class of service. JetBlue makes it simple with their Mint Class. Delta on the hand offers: Delta One, Delta Premium Select, First Class, Delta Comfort Plus. Which one of those is actually the business class you are looking for?

### International Business Class

When traveling across the ocean, many people assume they will be getting a lie flat seat in business class. This is not always the case. Many airlines are still flying planes with business class seats that are not fully flat. An airline like Etihad offers a world class Business Class, with unique seating configurations for couples and single travelers. Their cabins offer seats in both directions, so that you are not tripping over your neighbors feet. It is always important to ask your travel coordinator, to be sure you are getting the business class experience you are expect.
