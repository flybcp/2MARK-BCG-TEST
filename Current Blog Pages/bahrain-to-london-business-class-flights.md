## Types of Business Class Flights

There are different types of business class flights, and even though the prices are usually about the same, it is important to know the difference.

### Gulf Travelers Business Class

When traveling between the Gulf countries and Europe or Asia, most travelers assume they will be getting a lie flat seat in business class. This is not always the case. Many airlines are still flying planes with business class seats that are not fully flat. Airlines like Gulf Air, Emirates or Etihad offers a proper First Class & Business Class, with unique seating configurations for couples and single travelers. Their cabins offer seats in both directions, so that you are not tripping over your neighbors feet. If you are already flying business class, make sure you get your moneys worth, and speak with your travel coordinator, to be sure you are getting the right flying experience.

### European Business Class

European business class travelers knows that business class on an international flight from United Kingdom to Germany or Turkey many times is simply an economy seat, with the middle seat empty. That is not worth paying more, because it is better to reserve your seat and then select a window or aisle seat based on your personal preference.

When flying inside the continental United States, your flight could be 6 hours long, or as short as 30 minutes. For example; flying from EWR to LAX, the flight time is 6 hours. Major American Airlines have recently upgraded their planes, along with special names for Business Class. Each airline has their own unique names for the various class of service. JetBlue makes it simple with their Mint Class. Delta on the hand offers: Delta One, Delta Premium Select, First Class, Delta Comfort Plus. Which one is actually the business class you are looking for? Speak with your travel agent to get full details on the seat amenities.
